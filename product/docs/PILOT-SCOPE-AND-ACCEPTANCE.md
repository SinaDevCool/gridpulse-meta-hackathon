# Design-partner pilot scope and acceptance

## Pilot objective

Test whether GridPulse improves a real project's site decision, application preparation or flexible/staged connection discussion without making unsupported capacity claims.

## Pilot stages

1. Fit review and NDA.
2. Data request and evidence classification.
3. Candidate-site and project-maturity assessment.
4. Connection-strategy modelling.
5. Independent customer review.
6. Operator-engagement package.
7. Operator feedback capture where the customer authorises engagement.
8. Final decision memo and lessons learned.

## Acceptance criteria

- One genuine German connection case is used.
- Requested and minimum viable MW are declared.
- At least one candidate site is sufficiently identified.
- Customer supplies its available technical and operator evidence.
- GridPulse makes no available-capacity or connection-date claim without current operator evidence.
- Customer confirms whether the deliverables reduced rework, exposed a material risk or improved its next decision.

## Measures

Track assessment days, evidence gaps found, sites compared, operator questions prepared, clarification rounds, customer hours saved and any operator-validated change to strategy or timeline.
