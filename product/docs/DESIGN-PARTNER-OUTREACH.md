# Design-partner outreach

## Target

German data-centre, BESS or industrial-load developers with a live site or connection-strategy decision, preferably before or during operator pre-application engagement.

## Message

GridPulse is testing a bounded German Grid Connection Options Assessment. We structure project evidence, test firm, reduced-firm, flexible and staged operating hypotheses, and prepare an operator-question package. We do not claim available network capacity or replace the responsible operator's engineering study.

We are looking for one real case where the team is deciding between locations or connection configurations. The pilot is successful only if it exposes a material risk, reduces preparation work or improves the next decision.

## Discovery questions

1. What decision must be made and by when?
2. What requested and minimum viable import does the project require?
3. How many candidate sites or connection configurations remain open?
4. Which operator responses and technical documents exist?
5. What load or operating flexibility can be evidenced?
6. What has caused the most rework or uncertainty so far?
7. Who can independently review the deliverable?

## Commercial hypothesis

Offer a fixed-fee design-partner assessment with a defined scope, review workshop and anonymised-learning option. Do not make payment contingent on securing capacity or a particular operator outcome.
