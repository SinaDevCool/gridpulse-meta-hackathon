# Phase 3 validation protocol

## Objective

Establish whether GridPulse improves one real German connection decision without making an unsupported capacity, connection-point or energisation claim.

## Case acceptance

- A genuine German data-centre, BESS or large-load project.
- Requested and minimum viable import declared.
- At least two candidate locations, or one location with materially different connection configurations.
- Current customer evidence and operator correspondence available under NDA.
- A named customer decision owner and independent technical reviewer.

## Baseline

Before using GridPulse, record elapsed preparation time, known evidence gaps, current preferred route, unresolved operator questions and expected next decision date.

## Validation sequence

1. Classify every material input by source and validation status.
2. Upload a representative interval profile and record its hash and quality report.
3. Model requested-firm, reduced-firm, flexible and staged hypotheses.
4. Review operational consequences and minimum-viable failures.
5. Generate the operator package and management memo.
6. Conduct independent technical review.
7. Capture customer corrections and operator feedback.
8. Supersede assumptions rather than overwriting history.
9. Measure the decision change and preparation effort.

## Success measures

- At least one material evidence gap or risk found.
- Fewer customer clarification rounds, or a credible estimate of hours saved.
- A preferred option supported by a traceable rationale.
- Reviewer confirms facts, assumptions and operator gates are distinguishable.
- Customer confirms the output improved application preparation or the site/strategy decision.

## Failure conditions

- A modelled limit is presented as available capacity.
- A connection date is inferred without written operator evidence.
- The recommendation cannot be traced to inputs and evidence.
- Customer constraints make every modelled option unusable.
- The package adds work without improving a decision or exposing a risk.
