# German Grid Connection Options Assessment

## Intended customer

German data-centre, BESS and large-load developers deciding where and how to pursue a grid connection. The first commercial wedge is a data-centre or AI-infrastructure project with one to three candidate locations and a material target-date risk.

## Customer decision

Which location and connection strategy has the most credible evidence-backed route into formal network-operator engagement, and what must be resolved next?

## Included scope

1. Kickoff and customer information request.
2. Review of up to three customer-selected candidate locations.
3. Likely operator and published application-process screening.
4. Project-maturity and evidence-completeness assessment.
5. Comparison of requested-firm, reduced-firm, flexible and staged connection hypotheses.
6. Evidence-gap and operator-question register.
7. Versioned operator-engagement package.
8. Management decision memo with recommendation, limitations and actions.

## Exclusions

The assessment is not a power-flow study, grid-capacity confirmation, connection offer, capacity reservation, legal opinion, detailed design, permit, operator approval or real-time control service. Operator-specific capacity and dates remain unknown until confirmed in writing by the responsible network operator.

## Customer journey

Qualify project → provide candidate sites and data → classify evidence → compare sites → compare connection hypotheses → select preferred route → issue review package → coordinate operator questions → update decision record.

## Completion criteria

- All material inputs carry an evidence class and validation status.
- Each candidate site has an explainable maturity and evidence-gap assessment.
- The preferred route states its assumptions, limitations and operator-validation gates.
- The customer receives an operator package and management decision memo.
