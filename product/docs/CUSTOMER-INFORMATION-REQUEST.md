# Customer information request

## Commercial and programme

- Target energisation date and commercial deadline.
- Minimum viable initial MW and eventual requested MW.
- Development phases and load ramp.
- Consequence of delay or restriction.

## Location and maturity

- Coordinates, municipality and candidate-site list.
- Land ownership, option or control evidence.
- Planning and permitting status.
- Cable-route and substation-space status.
- Existing connection application identifiers.

## Technical

- Requested import/export and target voltage.
- Single-line diagram and equipment schedule.
- Interval load profile and forecast methodology.
- UPS, BESS, generation and cooling configuration.
- Critical load, shiftable load and redundancy requirements.

## Operator evidence

- Correspondence, meeting notes and named contacts.
- Application requirements and submissions.
- Connection-point, capacity, restriction or reinforcement statements.
- Any offer, study, milestone or cost estimate.

## Flexibility

- Minimum uninterrupted load.
- Static or dynamic import-limit tolerance.
- Notice period, duration and frequency tolerance.
- Workload-shifting and battery-control capability.
- Contractual or service-level restrictions.
