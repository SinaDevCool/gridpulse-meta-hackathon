## Root decisions

- Position GridPulse as grid-connection decision intelligence and governed capacity evidence for high-load properties, not a land-development GIS or operational-control platform.
- Extend `candidate_sites` as the property/assessment root. Reuse `project_connection_candidates`, `evidence_claims`, governed capacity results, reports, collaborations, activity and RLS.
- Treat property boundary and external parcel metadata as integration inputs. Do not recreate cadastral, ownership, environmental or general planning GIS.
- Preserve anonymous Finder state locally. Authenticated persistence must be optional, atomic, workspace-scoped and versioned.
- Missing, stale or failed capacity stays unavailable; no import path or report may normalize an absent MW value to zero.
- Remove public Activation and Operations pages and references. Preserve flexibility calculations, envelopes, governed studies and gated ID-based technical workspaces for future technical development.
