# Phase 1 pricing hypothesis

These are discovery prices, not published commitments. Confirm every engagement through a written proposal.

| Package                     | Indicative range | Intended scope                                                       |
| --------------------------- | ---------------: | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Single-site assessment      |   €5,000–€10,000 | One site, evidence review, strategy hypotheses, decision memo        |
| Multi-site assessment       |  €12,000–€25,000 | Up to three sites, comparison, strategy hypotheses, operator package |
| Operator-engagement support |  Scoped retainer | Meetings, evidence requests, package revisions and action tracking   |

For the first design partners, test a discounted fixed fee in exchange for structured feedback and permission to publish an anonymised case study. Do not make payment contingent on a capacity result that GridPulse cannot control.

Track sales-cycle length, willingness to pay, delivery hours, external engineering cost, gross margin and which deliverable drives the buying decision.
