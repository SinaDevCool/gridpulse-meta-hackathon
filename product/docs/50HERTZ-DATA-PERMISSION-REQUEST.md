# 50Hertz public-data reuse request

Status: prepared, not sent. Confirm the recipient and sending account before transmission.

## Suggested subject

Request to reuse and monitor public grid-connection information in GridPulse

## Draft

Dear 50Hertz team,

GridPulse is developing an evidence-led screening and workflow product for large electricity-demand projects in Germany. We help project teams organise public grid context, prepare connection enquiries, and distinguish clearly between public information, derived screening context, and written operator-confirmed evidence.

We would like permission to retrieve, cache, monitor for changes, and display selected factual information from your public grid-connection pages and downloadable resources, including source URL, publication date, retrieval date, and applicable limitations.

GridPulse would:

- attribute 50Hertz as the source and link to the original page;
- preserve source dates and show when our copy was last checked;
- avoid describing public context as available capacity, a connection offer, reservation, approval, or network study;
- remove or correct material promptly when requested;
- avoid reproducing logos or substantial copyrighted presentation unless separately permitted; and
- share the exact proposed fields and screenshots for review before public release.

Could you please confirm:

1. whether this metadata-level and factual reuse is permitted;
2. any licence, attribution wording, rate limit, or technical access conditions;
3. whether a machine-readable feed or preferred official endpoint is available; and
4. the appropriate contact for future data-quality or reuse questions?

We would be pleased to demonstrate the workflow and discuss how it could reduce incomplete large-load enquiries.

Kind regards,

GridPulse  
sina.khedmati@outlook.de
https://gridpulseinsights.com
